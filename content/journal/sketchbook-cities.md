- They open exactly the same businesses in the same district. At the mercado, there are 20 fresh juice stalls in a row, right next to each other. When they see me (obviously a tourist) passing by, they aggressively try to sell me their fresh juice, screaming like vampires hungry for blood, *“¡Señoritaaaaa! ¿Quieres jugo de frutaaaaaaaaaaaaas?!”* Rule 1: You must avoid eye contact with them at all costs, otherwise you'll never see the sunrise again.
- There's an original Inca market in Lima where they sell handicrafts and souvenirs, and then there are tons of souvenir shops selling exactly the same shit around that market.
- The examples just go on.
- Do they actually know the concept of cannibalization?
- My mind goes back to my childhood, when convenience stores were popping up all over our neighbourhood. Every 300 metres, you'd find another FamilyMart, Lawson, or 7-Eleven. My mother was cynically concerned about cannibalization, and that's when I first learned this fancy jargon.
- At first, I also wondered whether the government or municipality had introduced some sort of regulation requiring certain types of businesses to operate in specific districts. (This is indeed true for the mercado.)
- Or do people simply imitate the original business that became successful, hoping to duplicate that success by opening exactly the same shop in the same district?
- Then today, I learned a new business concept called Agglomeration economies. Yet, it's still too much the same business in one district.
- You always come across some weird customs when travelling in a foreign country anyway.
- And today, I went to a cosmetics shop selling local brands and blindly bought a solid conditioner advertised as 100% natural, only to realize later at home that it wasn't really natural at all. If this had happened in Germany, Germans would be raging in the Google reviews.
- The Peruvian way of doing business has either left me scratching my head or pissed me off so far. But that little picarones stall in Parque Kennedy is really good. You should try it.
