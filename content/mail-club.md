Hey, what was your passion when you were a kid?

I'm a child of the '80s, and when I was growing up, we were so relaxed about privacy that we casually published our names and addresses in magazines and newspapers to look for pen pals.

I loved writing letters to people I'd never met. When I was around 10, I had over 50 pen pals. I know, it's a crazy number, but that's how much I loved writing letters. And my mother absolutely exploded when she discovered I'd used up a whole bunch of stamps she'd been collecting for years. (Well, how didn't she suspect where I was resourcing all the costs anyway!)

When I learned to write in English at around 13, my world expanded beyond the shores of Japan, and I started exchanging letters with pen pals from Italy, Latvia, and several other corners of the world.

I loved writing letters page after page, talking about my life and asking about theirs, using beautiful letter paper and envelopes I'd personally picked out. Sometimes, I'd tuck in little treats and gifts for them, too.

I was obsessed with the sound of the mailman's bicycle. Whenever I heard him stop in front of our house, I'd peek out from behind my window and dash to the mailbox the moment he was gone.

Now, how many years has it been since I last sent a letter to a faraway friend? These days, we even break up with our significant others over text messages. Where is humanity going?!

There was something so magical about sending and receiving physical letters. And now, as an artist and a traveler, I want to bring that magic back. 💌

So I'm starting my YOLO Snail Mail Club!

Since my travel plans are constantly changing, my lovely sister will be helping me send the letters from Japan.

Every month, you'll receive a little piece of my world:

- 💌 A letter filled with stories from my travels
- 🌎 A postcard or art print inspired by wherever I happen to be
- 🎨 A seasonal illustration in the form of an art print or bookmark
- ✨ A YOLO omikuji (a little Japanese fortune-telling slip)

Think of it as having a pen pal who's always wandering somewhere in the world.

I'll draw illustrations inspired by wherever I happen to be and whatever catches my eye at the time, turning them into lovely cards, prints, and stickers. My sister will print, pack, and send everything from Japan, and a little piece of my world will make its way to your mailbox, wherever you are.

### As for what's next?

I'm planning to spend a few weeks in Japan, enjoying some quality time with my family and cats, before embarking on my next adventure in another country.

Who knows where life will take me this year?

That's the beauty of YOLO, isn't it?

I'd love to bring you along on this journey, one letter at a time. 💌🌎
