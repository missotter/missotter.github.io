About Me

I'm Atsuno Takase, a Japanese illustrator, artist, and storyteller based in Berlin, though I'm usually on the move, searching for the good, the bad, and everything in between that life has to offer.

I write and draw about my life, my thoughts, the people I encounter, and the little moments that make life feel more tangible — all through the lens of changing places and perspectives.

I love dancing and solitude, nature and quiet, animals and peculiar people. I'm currently working on a textile project, in search of the perfect pair of floral-print pants I've always dreamed of owning.
